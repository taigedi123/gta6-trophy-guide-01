    About this guide

    Platinum Road is an offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. It is a static page: it fetches nothing, sends nothing, and saves your progress in this browser only (localStorage). Export your progress to a file and import it back any time.

    How honest is it?

    The game released on 19 November 2026. Every trophy name, description and grade here is PlayStation's own, as Exophase and PSNProfiles listed it — the two agree on all 55. The guides are written in our own words from those official descriptions. Where a guide step is not pinned down yet — a location, a trigger, a count — the guide says so plainly instead of inventing one. Specific facts that come from other guides are cited beside the trophy they belong to; no guide text is copied from anywhere.
